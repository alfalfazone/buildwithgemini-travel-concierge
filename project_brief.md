# My agent: Travel Concierge (智慧旅遊與秘境規劃師)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps travelers discover hidden gems, design tailored daily itineraries, and manage trip plans with a catalog of curated destinations, local dining, and scenic spots.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers traveler preferences across sessions (travel style, budget level, dietary restrictions, preferred pace, companions/family status, past visited destinations).
- Tools: Look up destination attractions/restaurants by criteria, calculate travel route times, create/save itinerary items, and query weather forecasts.
- Catalog/UI: Collection of destinations, hotels, dining spots, and daily itinerary schedules rendered as A2UI rich cards and structured tables.
- Image gen: Generates scenic postcard-style visuals and aesthetic previews for recommended hidden gems and cultural attractions.
- Sandbox: Computes budget breakdowns, currency conversions, and travel pacing/timeline feasibility.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for budget/timeline optimization, Google Maps places/routes lookup, Cloud Trace
